# Firm founders do not need personal capital, and copying a technique is not equally hard for every technique

**Status:** partly - a firm's founder is now a member of a home-country stratum that holds savings: the stake moves from the stratum's purse (`ledger.transfer`, money conserved), the founder's personal capital is a share of the stratum's savings per member (`FOUNDER_WEALTH_MULTIPLE`), lenders need `ENTRY_EQUITY_SHARE` of the stake from the founder, and the copy chance falls with the founder stratum's literacy (`TACIT_SHARE_OF_COPYING`). Worlds without strata keep the pooled-capital rule. Test `firm_entry_capital`. Per-technique copy visibility is data: a node may declare `copy_visibility` (the share an onlooker can recover from the product or the yard, above 0 and at most 1) with a `copy_visibility_reason`, checked by `simulator.py validate` (`sim/engine/validate_copy_visibility.py`); `copy_difficulty` is its reciprocal, and a node that declares nothing keeps the count of trades and materials (labelled transitional). A first set of ten is filled (wheelbarrow, stirrup, horseshoe, hot air balloon, gunpowder, crucible and cementation steel, clear glass, spring steel, Hadfield steel) a second batch is filled across textiles, construction, metallurgy, chemicals, ships and others, and a third batch covers common materials, machine tools, engines, instruments, chemistry and electrical goods; count the nodes still lacking the field with `python3 -c` over `data.load()` nodes lacking `copy_visibility`. Test `copy_visibility`. Not done: filling the remaining nodes, and individual households with their own wealth (a stratum is a headcount with a purse); in Rome runs the strata's savings are so large that equity rarely binds, the crowding premium and exit do the bounding.

Raised by the owner (2026-10-02). Earlier Rome runs reached hundreds to thousands of firms. Firms enter from a pool of society's capital (`ENTREPRENEURIAL_CAPITAL_SHARE` of output, `sim/agents/registry.py` `consider_entry`) plus borrowing, not from particular people who have the money. A labourer cannot found a balloon works; a founder needs savings or a lender willing to back them, and only a few households in an ancient economy had that.

Copying is also not equally easy. A visible, simple device (a wheelbarrow, a hot-air balloon) can be copied by looking at it; a process whose essence is hidden (black powder's proportions and corning, steel's heat treatment, glass recipes) cannot be reverse-engineered from the product. Today copy difficulty comes from the count of trades and materials a node needs (`copy_difficulty`, `sim/engine/society_disclosure.py`).

What it would take: firm founders drawn from households that hold capital (the society's income and wealth distribution in data), each with its own savings and a lender's limit, so entry is bounded by who can actually fund it; and per-technique copy difficulty declared in data (how much of the know-how is visible in the product versus tacit or secret), with a stated basis, replacing the count of trades and materials.
