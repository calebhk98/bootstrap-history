# `money` shows a large aggregate "market will not absorb" deduction but names only one saturated concern

**Status:** open

At 1375 the ledger said the market would not absorb about 857 thousand pence a year of nominal output across concerns, yet only the rope walk was called out as directly saturated (about 73 percent of its tree quote). The larger deduction was not itemised per concern or per category, so the player could not tell which concerns to change. (Earlier, at 1320, the same ledger did itemise the loom and rope walk pair well; this is the gap at scale.)

Not replayed (needs a late game with many concerns). Related: 153 (closed: earnings differing between screens), 178 (market screen).

What it would take: under the deduction, one row per category (nominal, absorbed, lost, and the concerns in it) so the rows sum to the deduction, as the rest of the ledger already does.

Found in an England 1300 blind playtest (fog on, poor_scholar kit, 1300 to 1375). Reports: `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-tester-notes.md`, `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-yearly-log.md`; triage: `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-triage.md`.
