# Patron succession forces an unexplained automatic spend

**Type:** Agency / policy UX  
**Priority:** Medium

## Player evidence

Patron deaths automatically spent 800 denarii courting the heir, reduced protection, and increased scandal. This recurred at least twice.

## Why it matters

It is a material expenditure and strategic political choice, but it offers no decision point.

## Suggested change

Prompt for a choice, or add an `auto_court_heir` policy with a documented default.
