# Plague risk UI does not explain repeated annual staff-loss waves

**Type:** Risk communication / balance watch  
**Priority:** Medium

## Player evidence

The household suffered -14% staff in 166, 167, and 168 while the plague was active. The risk screen described a staff-loss percentage but did not make the repeated-wave cadence clear.

## Suggested change

State whether the percentage is per wave, per year, or total over the epidemic window, and expose expected duration/cumulative risk.
