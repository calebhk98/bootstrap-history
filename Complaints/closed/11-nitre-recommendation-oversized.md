# Recommended nitre-bed purchase is drastically oversized

**Type:** Recommendation/balance UX  
**Priority:** Medium

## Player evidence

With 1.1 t/year demand and 0.0008 t/m²/year yield, the game recommended `buy nitre 20000` (16 t/year). About 1,375 m² was sufficient in theory; 2,000 m² cleared the shortage.

## Suggested change

Size the recommendation from current deficit plus a stated safety buffer instead of suggesting a fixed 20,000 m² purchase.
