# What caps the number of firms in a niche is the rate of entry, because a firm's fixed costs are a tiny share of what a market pays

**Status:** closed - the number of firms follows what a firm carries against what the market pays, with no per-year entry cap and no per-parent cap on spinoffs. An entrant (and a spinoff) is founded one at a time while its expected takings, smoothed over years, less the concern's upkeep and wages and what a firm carries beat the market rate on its stake; a firm pays the same carrying costs every year, so they also drive exit. What a firm carries: a manager's hours (labelled span of control) and the rent of a site on its tile. Every concern now occupies a plot from its staff at a stated floor area per person (a labelled heuristic), plus the land its declared output works; the rent is the agent economy's land market's rent on the firm's tile (the home country's tile, since a firm has no tile of its own; the mean rent of land let where none was let on the tile), and the engine's land price while the agent economy is off. The cost of winning customers has a basis in the merchants' terms (`merchant_terms.agent_cost_per_tonne`: agents' hours per tonne a carrier lifts, at the merchant wage): a sale in the firm's own market needs no carrier and so costs nothing more, and where goods cross places the traders who carry them bear it, so a firm carries no separate term. The agent economy's own entry is uncapped (Complaint 398). Spinoffs: how many of a parent's staff consider leaving grows with staff and years, each founds a rival only if an entrant would earn (the same test and stake), and the ceiling is gone. Tests: `firm_count_from_costs` and `agents_spinoff` (small fixtures, run); `firm_count_whole_game` (slow topic, written, not run here: whole games are too slow in this container). Earlier measure (before these changes) with a driver that seeds 22 proven founder concerns in a Rome run (agent_economy off) and scales the economy's output factor by 5% a year: year 100 firms 871 before the first change, 387 after it.

A firm's takings are the founder's concern takings scaled by `economy ** ECONOMY_OUTPUT_SCALING_EXPONENT`, shared by every seller in the goods category. What a firm must carry (upkeep, wages, the copy cost of its know-how) does not scale with the economy, so by the time the economy has grown tens of times the costs are a few percent of one seller's takings. An entrant needs only a positive margin that beats the capital it ties up at the market rate, so entry stays profitable until a category is shared out among very many sellers; what actually stops the count is the one-entrant-per-proven-concern-per-year rate limit, not the market.

Incumbents now expand (Complaint 331), which lowers the count for the same market, but an entrant and an expanding incumbent see the same unit margin and the incumbent also pays for the price it lowers on its own earlier capacity, so entrants keep arriving wherever any unit margin remains.

What is missing is a cost a firm must carry whatever its size and that grows with the market it serves: management and administration (Complaint 331's span-of-control exponent is a labelled stand-in), the licences and levies a visible firm owes, the rent on a site, and the wage table following the economy (labour is the scarce input, so a richer economy pays more per hour; see `Complaints/283`, `319`). Once those exist the number of firms per niche follows from the market and the cost of a minimum efficient size, and the rate limit stops being the binding one.

Related: `330`, `331`, `321`, `181`, `341`.

Done (firm-costs-scale): `Sim.output_volume_scale()` is the one volume factor takings use, and `concern_running_scale` applies it to the upkeep of a concern that sells (founder, `venture_real_upkeep`, and actors, `SimWorld.upkeep`): inputs are bought per unit sold. `Sim.market_wage_per_hour` is the wage table times `labour_pay_scale()`, one figure for every employer; its share `LABOUR_PAY_SHARE_OF_OUTPUT_GAIN` is zero by default because above zero the founder's takeoff stalls (Complaint 354). With it at zero only upkeep follows the volume, so wages (a rising share of a firm's cost late) still lag it.
