# A credit freeze refuses starts the player can pay in cash, although its message says they are allowed

**Status:** closed - starts covered by cash in hand are allowed during a freeze

301 AD, 47,301 den cash, frozen until 303. `start scientific_method` (cost 1,106): "REFUSED: nobody here will fund new work ... until then you may finish what is running, and pay for something out of money you actually hold." `stuck`: "0 things you could begin, 0 of them you could pay for".

Also: the freeze date moved from 281 to 303 after a second credit exhaustion in 291; that is plausible, but `stuck` never explained that the earlier settlement ("INSOLVENCY SETTLED" in 279) left the player one bad year from a second freeze.

What it would take: allow starts fully covered by cash during a freeze (as the message says), or change the message to say nothing can be started.

Found in a new-player playtest (Rome 100 AD, poor_scholar kit, seed 1, played through `play --session`), report: `Complaints/reports/playtest-rome-seed1-new-player.md`.
