# The "SHORT OF GOLD" warning says your own workings cover demand when you have none

**Status:** closed - shortage remedy says to sink a working when none exists; the "already cover" clause prints only when a working of that material exists

577-581 AD every year: "SHORT OF GOLD: work running at 7% of plan. The market will not sell you enough gold, so you have to dig it: your own workings already cover the demand you have today, so this is the market, not you." `mines` listed no gold working; `capacity` showed gold capacity 0 against demand 0.30.

What it would take: print the "your own workings already cover" clause only when they exist.

Found in a new-player playtest (Rome 100 AD, poor_scholar kit, seed 1, played through `play --session`), report: `Complaints/reports/playtest-rome-seed1-new-player.md`.
