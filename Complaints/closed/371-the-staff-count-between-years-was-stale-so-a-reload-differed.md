# The firms' staff count between years was stale, so a reloaded game differed from the unbroken one

**Status:** closed - (the actor registry recounts staff when a year's actors are done, so the founder's turn reads what firms and governments employ now)

`sim/tests/test_save_round_trip_every_year.py` failed from year 31 of Rome seed 1 after the economy-from-production merge (each earlier merge passed it on its own). Stepping the unbroken game and a reloaded copy once from year 30 and comparing each phase showed the first difference in the founder's staff phase: `market_supply("artisan")` differed, because `actor_staff_fte` did (the unbroken game read less than the firms employed). The registry keeps per-trade running sums of actors' staff; entry and group formation change a firm's staff after the last actor has acted, and nothing recounted before the founder's next turn (the recount happens at the next year's first actor). A reloaded game builds a fresh registry and counts what is there, so it read the true figure. The stale figure was in the unbroken game only, not a missing save field, and it was a wrong value, not a harmless cache.

Resolution: `ActorRegistry.advance` calls `refresh_staff()` after entry and group formation. Tests: `sim/tests/test_complaint_371_actor_staff_between_years.py` compares the registry's count with the actual sum at the end of every year (fails on the old code from year 9); the every-year guard now passes. Unbroken results change from about year 10 (`python3 sim/perf_fingerprint.py check --quick`) because the pool the founder hires from no longer subtracts a stale firm count. Related: 320.
