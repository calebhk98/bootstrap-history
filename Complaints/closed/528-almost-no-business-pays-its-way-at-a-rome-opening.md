# Almost no business pays its way at a Rome opening

**Status:** closed - merged into 510 (the opening check, its measure and the owner's rule that a technology never earns); the design is 510 with 514

After 507 stage 3 a scan of the default Rome opening found almost no concern whose takings exceeded its running
cost. The owner's decision of 2026-10-19 withdrew the question as asked (a technology never earns; only plants and
firms selling what they make do), so what remains is a plant-level check at an opening, which depends on 510 and 514.
It and its measure (`python3 sim/simulator.py opening-checks --civs rome_100ad --concerns-only`) now live in 510.
