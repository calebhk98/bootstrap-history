# Completion messages name the society fields that changed but not by how much, where, or through what

**Status:** open

Germ theory "announced population and belief effects" (160 AD), movable type and newspaper subscriptions "explicitly raised literacy", antisepsis and vector control "changed population", vaccination changed population and state capacity:
the lines name fields, not before and after values. The tester could not attribute the literacy rise (18 to 21 percent, 21 to 32 percent) or the population step (about 16 percent over 5 years) and could not tell a prototype from nationwide access.
Closed 85 asked for goal-relevant deltas on completion and event messages and is closed; the tester's build shows the field names without the numbers, so either 85 was fixed for some messages only or they played an older build. Not replayed (needs a late game).

What it would take: for each effect line, old value, new value and the per-year change; say which part of the country the adoption curve covers. Related: 96 (education and demography screens), 116 (domain diffusion), 97 (why did this change).
