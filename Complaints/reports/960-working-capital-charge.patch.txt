Subject: [PATCH 1/2] Charge the return on working capital in the run cost
 producers plan with and the opening covers

A producer pays its wages and inputs before it sells; the money so tied up earns the year's rate lent out.
unit_cost.running_cost_per_run adds that charge (outlay times rate times the years of costs the cash target
holds) to the variable cost. producers.plan, share_that_pays, the break-even scale and the shedding margin use
it, and so do the opening's covering prices, working share and unit costs, so both read one function.
