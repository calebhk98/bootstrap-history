# PM triage of the open complaints (2026-10-19)

Scored against one release criterion from the owner: **the game ships when an AI agent can play a full
500-year game, in a reasonable time, without the game breaking.** Importance is weighted toward that;
difficulty is the work left to *close* the issue (a move from `open` to `partly` counts as no progress).

Scale: Difficulty 1 (an hour, data or one test) to 5 (multi-session redesign). Importance 1 (polish) to
5 (blocks release). List the files with `ls Complaints/*.md`; statuses with
`grep -m1 '^\*\*Status' Complaints/*.md`.

## Summary

- **The release criterion is bound by speed and by the opening.** 497 is the blocker. Behind it, the
  opening (526, 545, 473) is where most economic complaints converge: an opening at subsistence that
  drifts for decades makes every later number depend on when the spin-up stopped.
- **Duplication is the dominant architectural defect.** Five complaints are "two implementations of one
  thing": prices (507), businesses (514), food (537, 549), debt (555), labour hours (556). Each is a
  place a fix lands on one side and is invisible on the other.
- **The multiplayer cluster (481 to 496) is mostly independent of the economy** and can proceed in
  parallel; 483 and 494 to 496 are the foundations the rest stand on.
- **Cheap closes** (data or one check): 541, 535, 534, 531, 560, 504.
