# Request: open a venture in another town or region for a separate demand pool

**Status:** open

Market saturation between concerns in one category (a loom and a rope walk both selling textiles) was praised, and the tester suggested the natural response: deliberately open a concern in another town or region, paying logistics and management costs in exchange for a distinct demand pool. Related is the wish for regional diffusion: possessing a technology nationally should differ from deploying it across England, and "personal discovery, local adoption, civilisation-wide diffusion" should be more clearly separate.

Not a defect. Overlaps 138 (reach ignores transport), 140 (regions and tiles are two maps), 111 (urbanisation), 116 (domain-specific diffusion). Needs a location on a concern, per-location demand, and a transport and supervision cost through the normal rules; do not special-case a civilisation.

Found in an England 1300 blind playtest (fog on, poor_scholar kit, 1300 to 1375). Reports: `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-tester-notes.md`, `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-yearly-log.md`; triage: `Complaints/reports/playtest-england-1300-fog-triage.md`.
