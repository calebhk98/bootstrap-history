# Request: education institutions between "hire one scholar" and a full school

**Status:** open

The gap between hiring one scarce scholar (whose wage rises for the whole category when hired) and a school costing several hundred thousand pence is "very binary", in the tester's words. Asked for a ladder: tutoring circles, scriptoria, guild schools, cathedral schools, bursaries, translation programmes, apprenticeships, textbooks and professional communities. The gap was a central campaign problem through 1375: literacy stayed near 6 percent and scholar or scribe supply set the pace of most mathematics.

Not a defect, a content and mechanics request. Related: 96 (education and demography screens), 87 (closed: overloaded "school"), 114, 116. Implement as data with the node `mechanics` field (`data/branches/MECHANICS.md`) acting through the normal labour and literacy rules, not as special cases (CLAUDE.md 4.3).
