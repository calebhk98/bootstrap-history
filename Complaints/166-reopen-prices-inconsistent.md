# Reopening a shut concern has three different prices, none stated up front

**Status:** open

- After a staff-loss closure: "reopening soon costs a tenth of what opening did" (closure message).
- After `mothball`, `open <id>` charged 1x yearly upkeep (nitre_beds: 10,781).
- After `mothball`, `restore <id>` charged 2x (nitre_beds: 21,561; refractory_fireclay: 9,433 against 4,717 upkeep).
`help mothball` ("Stops its upkeep; restore reopens it") and `help restore` ("Undoes mothball") mention no cost, nor does the `mothball` reply.

What it would take: one reopening rule, or at least the cost printed in the `mothball` reply and in `help restore`.

Found in a new-player playtest (Rome 100 AD, poor_scholar kit, seed 1, played through `play --session`), report: `Complaints/reports/playtest-rome-seed1-new-player.md`.
