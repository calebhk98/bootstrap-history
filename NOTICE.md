# Notice

## Eurace@Unibi

Parts of the economy follow the Eurace@Unibi agent-based macroeconomic model by Herbert Dawid, Simon Gemkow,
Philipp Harting, Sander van der Hoog and Michael Neugart (Bielefeld University), source at
https://github.com/ETACE/eurace_unibi, used with the authors' permission given to the project owner. The rules are
re-implemented in Python for this simulation and changed to fit it; each function or constant that follows Eurace
names the Eurace source file and function it follows in its own comment. Find them with `grep -rn -i eurace sim`.

Model references:
- Dawid, H., Harting, P., van der Hoog, S. and Neugart, M. (2018), "Macroeconomics with heterogeneous agent models:
  Fostering transparency, reproducibility and replication", Journal of Evolutionary Economics.
- Dawid, H., Gemkow, S., Harting, P., van der Hoog, S. and Neugart, M. (2018), "Agent-based macroeconomic modeling and
  policy analysis: The Eurace@Unibi Model", The Oxford Handbook of Computational Economics and Finance, ch. 17,
  pp. 490-519 (as cited in the repository's README).
